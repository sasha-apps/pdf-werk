Merge PDF on iPhone

No app needed, right in Safari: combine PDFs from the Files app, iCloud Drive or a mail attachment into one file.

    Your files are not uploaded. Everything happens right in your browser.

    How it works on iPhone and iPad

    1. Tap the box above. iOS opens a picker; tap Browse to reach the Files app and iCloud Drive.
    2. Choose the PDFs. They appear in the list, and the arrows change the order.
    3. Tap Merge PDFs, then the download. Safari saves the file in the Downloads folder of the Files app.

    FAQ

    Do I need an app?

    No, Safari is enough. Chrome and Firefox on iPhone work too.

    And on Android?

    Same steps: open the page in Chrome, choose files, merge. The result lands in your Downloads folder.

    Check it yourself: nothing is uploaded

    Open a tool, then switch off Wi-Fi or put your phone in airplane mode and use it. It still works, because the processing runs on your device. A service that uploads your file to a server would fail without internet.
