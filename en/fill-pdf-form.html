Fill in a PDF form

Fill in applications and forms on your computer or phone and save them as PDF.

    Your files are not uploaded. Everything happens right in your browser.

    How to fill in a PDF form

    1. Choose the form. All fields appear as input boxes.
    2. Fill them in. Hover over a field label to see its full name.
    3. Choose whether the fields stay editable and click Save filled PDF.

    FAQ

    No fields are found. Why?

    Then the PDF is not a fillable form, just a printed form saved as a file. Or it uses XFA, an older format some official forms use that only Adobe Reader understands.

    What does "flatten" mean?

    Your entries become a fixed part of the page. Nobody can change them in the form afterwards, and the file looks the same in every program.

    Is the form uploaded?

    No. Forms often contain tax IDs, bank details or health data. Here everything stays in your browser.

    Check it yourself: nothing is uploaded

    Open a tool, then switch off Wi-Fi or put your phone in airplane mode and use it. It still works, because the processing runs on your device. A service that uploads your file to a server would fail without internet.
