Turn screenshots into a PDF

Take a screenshot, paste it here with Ctrl+V, done. Handy for orders, chats and receipts.

    Your files are not uploaded. Everything happens right in your browser.

    The quickest way

    1. Take a screenshot to the clipboard:
      • Windows: Windows + Shift + S, then select the area
      • Mac: Cmd + Ctrl + Shift + 4
    2. Click on this page and press Ctrl + V (Mac: Cmd + V). The screenshot appears in the list.
    3. Repeat for more screenshots, then click Create PDF.

    On a phone, simply choose the screenshots from your photo library above.

    For long chat screenshots choose One image per page and page size Same as the image, so nothing gets scaled down.

    Check it yourself: nothing is uploaded

    Open a tool, then switch off Wi-Fi or put your phone in airplane mode and use it. It still works, because the processing runs on your device. A service that uploads your file to a server would fail without internet.
