Merge PDF

Combine several PDFs into one file, in the order you choose.

    Your files are not uploaded. Everything happens right in your browser.

    How to merge PDFs

    1. Drop two or more PDF files into the box or click it to choose them.
    2. Put them in the right order with the arrows.
    3. Click Merge PDFs and download the result.

    FAQ

    Are my files uploaded?

    No. The PDFs are processed in your browser and never leave your computer or phone, so the tool is fine for invoices, contracts and job applications.

    How many PDFs can I merge?

    There is no fixed limit. With very large files it depends on your device's memory.

    Do links and quality survive?

    Pages are copied unchanged, without recompression. Text stays searchable.

    Does it work on a phone?

    Yes, on iPhone, iPad and Android in any current browser.

    Check it yourself: nothing is uploaded

    Open a tool, then switch off Wi-Fi or put your phone in airplane mode and use it. It still works, because the processing runs on your device. A service that uploads your file to a server would fail without internet.
