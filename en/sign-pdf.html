Sign PDF

Sign with mouse, pen or finger and put the signature into the PDF, without printing and scanning.

    Sign here with mouse or finger

    Your files are not uploaded. Everything happens right in your browser.

    How to sign a PDF

    1. Choose the PDF.
    2. Sign in the box: with your finger on a phone, with mouse or touchpad on a laptop.
    3. Choose position, size and page. Optionally add a line such as place and date.
    4. Click Add signature and download the signed PDF.

    Is it legally valid?

    A drawn signature placed in a PDF is a simple electronic signature. For many everyday documents that is enough, for example quotes, order confirmations or forms where the recipient accepts it. Where the law requires a handwritten signature on paper, it is not. If in doubt, ask the recipient.

    General information, not legal advice.

    FAQ

    Is my signature stored?

    No. It only exists while the page is open and is never sent anywhere. Reloading the page removes it.

    Check it yourself: nothing is uploaded

    Open a tool, then switch off Wi-Fi or put your phone in airplane mode and use it. It still works, because the processing runs on your device. A service that uploads your file to a server would fail without internet.
