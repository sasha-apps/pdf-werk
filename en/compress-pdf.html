Compress PDF

Make large PDFs, such as scans, small enough for email or an upload field.

    Your files are not uploaded. Everything happens right in your browser.

    How to compress a PDF

    1. Choose the PDF.
    2. Choose the level. Medium fits most cases.
    3. Click Compress PDF. You see right away how much smaller the file got.

    How does it work?

    Every page is saved again as an image at a lower resolution. This works best for scans and phone photos, which are often far larger than needed: a 300 dpi scan has about nine times as many pixels as a 100 dpi one.

    Good to know: afterwards the text is part of an image. It can no longer be selected, copied or searched, and links stop working. For applications, receipts and ID copies that doesn't matter. For a document people need to search, keep the original.

    FAQ

    My file doesn't get smaller. Why?

    PDFs that are mostly text are already very small. Turning them into images doesn't help, and the tool tells you so.

    Check it yourself: nothing is uploaded

    Open a tool, then switch off Wi-Fi or put your phone in airplane mode and use it. It still works, because the processing runs on your device. A service that uploads your file to a server would fail without internet.
