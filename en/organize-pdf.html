Organize PDF pages

See all pages as thumbnails, drag them into the right order, rotate or delete single pages.

    Your files are not uploaded. Everything happens right in your browser.

    How to organize PDF pages

    1. Choose the PDF. All pages appear as small previews.
    2. On a computer, drag a page to its place. On a phone, use the arrows.
    3. Use ⟳ to rotate a single page and ✕ to remove it.
    4. Click Save new PDF.

    FAQ

    Does quality suffer?

    No. The thumbnails are only for viewing; the new PDF contains the original pages, unchanged and searchable.

    Check it yourself: nothing is uploaded

    Open a tool, then switch off Wi-Fi or put your phone in airplane mode and use it. It still works, because the processing runs on your device. A service that uploads your file to a server would fail without internet.
