Split PDF

Split one PDF into several files or pull out single pages.

    Your files are not uploaded. Everything happens right in your browser.

    How to split a PDF

    1. Choose the PDF.
    2. Enter the page ranges you want as separate files, or choose Every page as its own file.
    3. Click Split PDF and download the parts one by one or as a ZIP.

    FAQ

    How do I write page ranges?

    1-3 means pages 1 to 3, 5 only page 5, 8- page 8 to the end. Separate several ranges with commas.

    Is the PDF uploaded?

    No, it is processed in your browser and stays on your device.

    Check it yourself: nothing is uploaded

    Open a tool, then switch off Wi-Fi or put your phone in airplane mode and use it. It still works, because the processing runs on your device. A service that uploads your file to a server would fail without internet.
